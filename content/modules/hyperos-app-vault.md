## Overview

Xiaomi's App Vault (`com.miui.personalassistant`) serves as the swipe-left auxiliary screen on HyperOS and MIUI launchers, hosting interactive widgets, device shortcuts, health metrics, and information cards. On budget and mid-tier devices, Xiaomi commonly strips background blur effects—replacing them with flat grey or opaque panels—and hides advanced interactive widgets reserved for flagship handsets.

Modified by developer Kashi through Mods-Center, **HyperOS-App-Vault** is a systemless enhancement module that installs a feature-unlocked edition of the application based on optimized Xiaomi.eu builds.

## Key Enhancements

- **Frosted Glassy Blur**: Restores dynamic background blur for widget containers, allowing wallpaper tones to filter softly behind cards.
- **High-End Feature Spoofing**: Includes an internal toggle that identifies the handset as a top-tier Xiaomi flagship, unlocking hidden widget designs, complex data visualizers, and battery monitor tiles.
- **Blur Customization**: Adjust and fine-tune blur opacity and radius directly within App Vault settings.
- **Fluid Feed Animations**: Eliminates interface stutter when swiping into and out of the App Vault panel from the default launcher.

## Installation Guidelines

1. **Android 14+ Prerequisite**: If running Android 14 or above on Global or China ROMs, flash the **Apk Protection Patch** first to allow modified system packages.
2. Download `HyperOS_AppVaultV*.zip` from the project's releases.
3. Open **Magisk Manager** or **KernelSU**, install the zip archive, and reboot.
4. Swipe left from your home screen or long-press your wallpaper to access the enriched widget collection.
